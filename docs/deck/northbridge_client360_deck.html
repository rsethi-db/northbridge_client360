The problem

Kestrel Industries exists 6 times in 4 systems, and nobody can say what it's worth

  • Harbor & ColeKESTREL INDS PLC
  • Harbor & ColeKESTREL INDUSTRIES PLC
  • NB BillingKestrel Industries Public Limited Company
  • NB CRMKestrel Industries PLC
  • NB CRMKestrel Industries plc
  • PlacementKestrel Industries plc

The same client, as held today. One H&C record has a transposed registration number; one CRM duplicate has none.

What that costs

  • Renewals at risk. Two account teams pitch the same CFO without knowing it.
  • Cross-sell missed. Harbor & Cole clients never see Northbridge specialty lines.
  • Revenue leaks. Carrier statements cannot be matched to the client who was billed.
  • Compliance exposure. Defence & government clients copied into every system and spreadsheet.
  • Integration stalls. "Wait for the CRM migration" is a two-year answer.

Decisions and trade-offs

Why this design, and what we chose not to do

DecisionWhyTrade-off
Governed layer above the sourcesValue now; no source system has to winSources stay messy; we standardise downstream
Explainable GBM + LLM for the grey zoneAuditable features; AI only where it adds judgementNeeds labelled history to start (stewards supply it)
ABAC on tags, not per-table filtersNew tables governed by taggingRelies on tagging discipline
Restricted clients stay in the lakehouseLakebase cannot sync policy-protected tables; data minimisationRestricted views query the warehouse (~3 s, not ms)
Snapshot sync to LakebaseSmall tables, simple, consistentMinutes, not seconds, to refresh; move to continuous at scale

Questions we expect

Cost, risk and integration

Business
  • "We already have an MDM programme." This feeds it: a governed golden record and steward workflow today, published to any MDM later.
  • "What does it cost?" Serverless, pay per use; the pilot scope is a small fraction of one value lever.
  • "Can we trust automatic merges?" Every merge has a confidence, a lineage trail and a steward override.
Technical
  • Scale. Blocking keeps comparisons near-linear; featurisation moves to Spark UDFs beyond ~1M records.
  • Integration. Lakeflow Connect for Salesforce / SQL Server; the golden record syncs back to operational systems.
  • Security. ABAC, on-behalf-of-user app and Genie access, service principal reads gold only.
Every answer on this slide is demonstrated in the running build, not promised.
